Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Applies to the Deltix Network app and deltixllc.com

Quick summary: Energy powers your daily activity in the app; $DLTX is the network's utility and reward token. All reward amounts, caps, and rates below are protocol parameters — they can evolve over time through Deltix DAO governance, and this page is updated when they do.

⚡ Energy & Daily Rewards

What is Energy and how do I earn it?

Energy is the in-app resource used to play instant Energy games, unlock cosmetics, and start Deltix Earn sessions. You can earn it by:

  • Watching rewarded ads — 1 ⚡ per ad, up to 100 ⚡ per day. Ads are always optional.
  • The Daily Ladder — up to 23 ⚡ per day (see below).
  • Deltix Rain, the Weekly Calendar, the Spin Wheel, the Daily Chest, the Deltix Tree, missions, and puzzles — smaller daily amounts.

Free (non-ad) Energy sources share a combined daily allowance of 50 ⚡ so the economy stays fair for everyone.

How much Energy does one ad give?

Exactly 1 ⚡ per rewarded ad, up to 100 ⚡ per day. During special 2× bonus hours, ad rewards are doubled — that's intentional, not a bug.

How does the Daily Ladder work?

The ladder has 15 steps that must be claimed in order and reset every day (UTC). Step 1 is free; each further step is unlocked by one optional rewarded ad. Every step pays 1 ⚡, except the starred bonus steps 10 and 15, which pay 5 ⚡ each — a maximum of 23 ⚡ per day.

How does the free Spin Wheel work?

You get a free spin every 12 hours. The wheel holds 2 ⚡, 5 ⚡, and 8 ⚡ segments, small $DLTX prizes (0.25 / 0.5 / 1), and a rare 5 $DLTX jackpot. Every prize shown on the wheel is genuinely winnable — the jackpot is simply rare.

How does Flip the Coin work?

Flip the Coin costs 30 ⚡ per flip. A winning flip pays between 25 and 35 ⚡; some flips pay nothing. Over time the game is a fun risk, not a way to generate unlimited Energy.

◆ $DLTX Rewards

How much $DLTX can I earn per day?

Play & Earn activity (arcade wins, check-in, mystery box, spin, chest, puzzles, missions) shares a combined cap of 10 $DLTX per day. Deltix Earn sessions (12 hours each) can add up to another 10 $DLTX per day. Staking rewards accrue separately from your staked balance.

Will game and staking rewards continue as the network grows?

Yes. The network launched with a genesis supply of 100,000,000 $DLTX, and the protocol issues up to 5% of supply per year, which funds staking rewards. Game rewards are minted within strict per-account daily caps. Both reward streams are part of the protocol's ongoing design — growth of the distributed supply does not switch them off.

Can reward rates be paused, reduced, or changed?

Reward rates, daily caps, and the issuance rate are protocol parameters, and the whitepaper defines them as governance surfaces of the Deltix DAO. They can be tuned over time to keep the economy healthy. Two things never change retroactively:

  • $DLTX already earned and in your wallet is never taken away.
  • Parameter changes only affect future earning, and are visible on the public chain.
Is the 5% annual issuance fixed forever?

No — 5% is the current protocol parameter, not a permanent constant. It can be raised or lowered through a governance decision. Any change is deliberate and transparent: it goes through the DAO process and takes effect openly, never silently.

🔥 Burning & Supply

How does $DLTX burning work?

Deltix uses EIP-1559-style fee burning — burned tokens are permanently removed from total supply:

  • Transfers: every peer-to-peer send burns a 0.1% fee (minimum 0.01 $DLTX).
  • Unstaking: withdrawing a stake burns 1% of the principal.

Net supply change = issuance − burns, so high network activity pushes the supply toward deflation.

Why does the total burned amount look small right now?

Burn volume scales with network activity — transfers and unstaking — not with time. In a young network, activity (and therefore burning) starts small and grows with usage. That is the expected behavior of an activity-based burn, not a malfunction.

Can the burn rate be increased?

Yes. The transfer-fee and unstake-fee rates are protocol parameters, listed in the whitepaper as governance surfaces. If the community decides burning should be more aggressive as the network matures, the rates can be raised through the DAO process.

🗳 Governance & Trust

Who decides the economic parameters?

The Deltix DAO. Every staked $DLTX equals one vote, and the whitepaper defines issuance, fees, reward rates, and caps as explicit governance surfaces. Governance scope expands in phases — from parameter voting today toward full on-chain control.

Is the supply allocation fair?

The core-team allocation is hard-capped at 20% of distributed supply, enforced in code — at least 80% always belongs to the community. Every mint, transfer, reward, and burn is sealed into the public Deltix chain and can be inspected in the in-app explorer.

Does $DLTX have monetary value?

No. $DLTX is an in-app utility and reward token with no monetary value. It is not redeemable for real-world currency or other cryptocurrency, and it is not an investment product. Rewards are variable protocol incentives and are never guaranteed.
