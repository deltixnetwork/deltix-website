Delete Your Account

Applies to the Deltix Network app (package network.deltix.app) by Deltix Network

You can permanently delete your Deltix Network account and associated data at any time, using either of the methods below.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open the Deltix Network app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

  1. Email support@deltixllc.com with the subject "Account deletion request", from the email address registered to your account.
  2. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, then confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and account record
  • Your wallet, $DLTX balance, staking positions, and reward history
  • Your governance votes and arcade activity linked to your account
  • Any active sign-in sessions

What is kept

  • Anonymous transaction records already written to the public Deltix chain. These contain only transaction identifiers and amounts — never your email address or any personal information — and are immutable by design.
  • Nothing else. There is no additional retention period beyond the 30-day processing window for email requests.
Deletion is permanent. Your $DLTX balance and staking positions cannot be recovered. $DLTX has no monetary value, so no funds are lost.

Questions

Contact support@deltixllc.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
